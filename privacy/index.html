Citizenship Prep

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Summary

Citizenship Prep does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Your progress

Your state, test dates, answers and study progress are stored only on your device. We have no server, so we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Speech

Questions are read aloud by your device's built-in voice. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Network

The app connects only to Apple for App Store purchases. It works fully offline.

Purchases

Subscriptions and passes are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the U.S. naturalization interview and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Not a government service

Citizenship Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or any government agency. Questions and answers are published by USCIS at uscis.gov.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
